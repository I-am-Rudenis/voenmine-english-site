Server rules

6.1
If you call a Moderator/Administrator, justify why they should teleport to you. If you simply write “Mod TP”, the Moderator has the right not to teleport to you and to ignore your message. Frequent messages are treated as flooding and are punished under the corresponding rule.
6.2
If you find the belongings of another player who has lost their items in some way, you must keep them by taking them into your inventory or putting them in a chest at the scene, and then return them to the owner. If the owner is not found within a day, you may keep the resources.
6.3
If a player has broken the rules of an event/RP segment, they may choose the punishment: either according to the server rules or according to the rules of the event/RP.
6.4
Regardless of the fine amount indicated by the player on a sign, for damaging/stealing someone else’s property and other offenses, the fine amount is set at the discretion of the Administration in the complaint.
6.5
Building and/or using chunk loaders is prohibited.
6.6
It is prohibited by any means to copy or pass off as your own someone else’s intellectual property on the server, including: — artworks [copying maps, coming to someone else’s artwork and creating your own maps, etc.] — buildings [any buildings, including farms and other mechanisms]
6.7
Concealing violations and/or deceiving the administration in any form, as well as covering for violators, is prohibited.
6.8
Any attempts to hack other people’s accounts on the server are prohibited.
