VoenMine Administration Rules

1.0 This section of the rules applies to all members of the project administration.
1.1 In this section of the rules, Moderators are people holding the rank of Helper or higher.
1.2 A server moderator must be literate, well-mannered, understanding and tolerant towards all players of the project.
1.3 A moderator must observe censorship and speech norms when communicating with players.
1.4 A moderator must always take screenshots of a player’s violation, but is not obliged to provide them to players.
1.5 A moderator must be able to account for their actions to any member of the technical administration.
1.6 The main duty of a moderator is to control the gameplay and to prevent in good time various incidents that may lead to a violation of the whole set of server rules.
1.7 A moderator must cite the relevant clause of these rules when enforcing them.
1.8 A moderator must carry out the administration’s instructions (related to the project) on time.
1.9 A moderator is forbidden to cause harm/damage to a player unless the situation requires it.
1.10 A moderator has no right to interfere with/teleport/kick players (including other moderators) without a serious reason.
1.11 A moderator is forbidden to moderate/administrate/play on other game projects.
1.12 A moderator has no right to abuse their powers for the benefit of themselves, friends or third parties.
1.13 A moderator has no right to arrange exchanges or trade, or to supply players (including relatives) with resources if those resources were confiscated from another player or were obtained using capabilities available only to a moderator.
1.14 A moderator may take time off for no more than 14 days within a month. Trainees cannot take time off, as they are undergoing training. Trainees who want time off will be temporarily removed from their position (so there is no point in applying if you need to be away in a few days).
1.15 If the rules are violated, the moderator’s account is temporarily reset pending investigation.
1.16 A moderator may be removed from their position if they sow discord in the team and behave inappropriately towards players and colleagues, even outside the project.
1.17 A moderator is forbidden to disclose to outsiders the contents of private Administration/Moderation conferences, etc., or private conversations with members of the Administration/Moderation, etc. To disclose messages from a private conversation, permission must be obtained from the other party. The exception is situations where the text may serve as evidence of a violation of these rules. Outsiders are all those who do not take part in the conference or private conversation.
1.18 Project moderators are forbidden to conceal misconduct of any players or moderators.
1.19 A moderator may be removed from their position if they keep resources obtained through a bug or dupe.
1.20 A moderator may be removed from their position if they were absent without a valid reason for more than 7 days, or for a prolonged period of low online activity.
1.21 For violating any rule in this set, a moderator will receive at minimum a reprimand, and at maximum will be removed.
