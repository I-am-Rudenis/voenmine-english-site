VoenMine Administration Rules

1.0 This section of the rules applies to all members of the project administration.
1.1 In this section, Moderators are people with the rank of Helper or higher.
1.2 A server Moderator must be competent, well-mannered, understanding, and tolerant toward all project players.
1.3 A Moderator must observe censorship and appropriate language standards when communicating with players.
1.4 A Moderator must always take screenshots of a player violation, but is not required to provide them to players.
1.5 A Moderator must be able to report their actions to any member of the technical administration.
1.6 The Moderator’s main duty is to monitor gameplay and promptly prevent incidents that could lead to violations of the server rules.
1.7 A Moderator must cite the relevant rule when enforcing it.
1.8 A Moderator must promptly follow administration instructions related to the project.
1.9 A Moderator must not harm or damage a player unless the situation requires it.
1.10 A Moderator may not interfere with, teleport, or kick players (including other Moderators) without a serious reason.
1.11 A Moderator may not moderate, administer, or play on other gaming projects.
1.12 A Moderator may not abuse their powers for their own benefit or for the benefit of friends or third parties.
1.13 A Moderator may not trade, exchange, or supply players (including relatives) with resources confiscated from another player or obtained using Moderator-only abilities.
1.14 A Moderator may take leave for no more than 14 days during a month. Trainees may not take leave because they are undergoing training. If a trainee needs leave, they will be temporarily removed from the position.
1.15 If the rules are violated, the Moderator account is temporarily reset pending investigation.
1.16 A Moderator may be removed if they cause discord within the team or behave inappropriately toward players or colleagues, even outside the project.
1.17 Модератору запрещено разглашать посторонним лицам содержание частной конференции Администрации/Модерации и т.д., личных переговоров с членами Администрации/Модерации и т.д. Для разглашения сообщений из личной беседы необходимо получить разрешение у собеседника. Исключение составляют ситуации, когда текст может служить свидетельством нарушения настоящих правил. Посторонними лицами признаются все, кто не участвует в конференции или личной беседе.
1.18 Project Moderators are prohibited from concealing misconduct by any players or Moderators.
1.19 A Moderator may be removed if they keep resources obtained through a bug or duplication exploit.
1.20 A Moderator may be removed for an unjustified absence of more than 7 days or prolonged low activity.
1.21 For violating any rule in this code, a Moderator will receive at least a reprimand and may ultimately be removed from the position.
